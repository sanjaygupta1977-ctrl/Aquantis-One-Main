# 10 MLD MBR STP - Detailed Project Report (DPR)

## Executive Summary

A 10 MLD capacity Membrane Bioreactor (MBR) Sewage Treatment Plant with advanced water reuse capability, designed for urban wastewater treatment with tertiary effluent quality.

---

## 1. Project Overview

### 1.1 Basic Parameters
- **Design Capacity**: 10 Million Liters Per Day (MLD)
- **Treatment Type**: MBR (Membrane Bioreactor)
- **Effluent Quality**: Tertiary treated, suitable for reuse
- **Location**: Urban area (Bangalore example)
- **Coordinates**: 12.9716°N, 77.5946°E
- **Technology**: Advanced biological + membrane filtration

### 1.2 Key Features
- Integrated biological treatment with membrane separation
- No secondary settling tank required
- High quality effluent (suitable for reuse)
- Compact footprint compared to conventional ASP
- Advanced automation & monitoring

### 2.2 Treatment Structures (Volume Calculation)

#### Pre-Treatment
- **Inlet Chamber**: 50 m³
  - Flow distribution & measurement
  - Automatic screening

- **Grit Chamber**: 120 m³
  - HRT: 5 minutes at peak flow
  - Grit removal for protection

#### Primary Treatment
- **Primary Settling Tank**: 250 m³
  - SOR: 1.5 m/hour
  - Removes 40-60% suspended solids
  - Sludge raking system

#### Biological Treatment
- **Aeration Tank (MBR)**: 1,500 m³
  - SRT: 15-20 days
  - MLSS: 8,000-10,000 mg/L
  - Fine bubble aeration
  - Membrane module submerged

#### Tertiary Treatment
- **Membrane Filter Housing**: 150 m³
  - UF membrane (MBR) integrated in aeration tank
  - Filtration area: 500 m²
  - Pore size: 0.4 µm

- **Chlorine Contact Tank**: 80 m³
  - CT concept: 30 minutes contact time
  - Chlorination for disinfection

#### Sludge Treatment
- **Sludge Thickening Tank**: 100 m³
  - Gravity thickening
  - MLSS increase from 8,000 to 40,000 mg/L

- **Sludge Dewatering**: 50 m³
  - Belt filter press feed tank
  - Sludge disposal preparation

- **Filtration Area**: 500 m²
- **Pore Size**: 0.4 µm (Ultrafiltration)
- **Configuration**: Submerged in aeration tank
- **Flux**: 15-20 LMH (Liters per square meter per hour)
- **MLSS**: 8,000-10,000 mg/L

- **Supplier Standards**: ISO 9001, ISO 14001
- **Unit Cost**: ₹5,000,000 (₹10,000/m²)
- **Total**: ₹5,000,000

**Membrane Justification:**
- 0.4 µm pore size removes bacteria & viruses
- Tertiary effluent quality without sand filtration
- Suitable for reuse (irrigation, toilet flushing, cooling)
- 5-7 year replacement cycle
- Regular chemical cleaning required
